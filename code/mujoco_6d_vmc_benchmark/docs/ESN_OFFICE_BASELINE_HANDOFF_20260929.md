## MuJoCo apparatus

The scene is generated by
`scripts/office_complex_scene_v5_20260917.py` and evaluated by
`scripts/validate_office_apparatus_v5_20260917.py`.

The world contains:

1. A real desk and a thick L-shaped corner: a vertical face plus a horizontal
   top support. In `--corner-placement`, the payload destination is
   approximately `[0.565, -0.302, 0.545]` and support is checked through the
   `office_v5_corner_top` contact geom.
2. A finite-mass hollow cup with 20 wall boxes, base cylinder, capsule handle,
   free joint and desk support. `combined-contact-v2` adds a small contact
   handle for an auditable brush; this is simulator geometry, not actor input.
3. A bolted cylindrical pillar (`office_v5_pillar` plus
   `office_v5_pillar_mount`). The original combined-v4 pillar is centered near
   `[0.585, -0.300, 0.700]`, radius `15 mm`, half-height `25 mm`, with low
   friction material. It lies near the carry/retreat corridor and is the source
   of the combined-v4 blockage.
4. A free ball with one reset-time initial velocity, restitution contacts with
   floor/desk, and delayed launch at `2 s` in the current formal reference.
5. A finite-mass articulated person surrogate: torso, head, legs, shoulder,
   elbow, compliant wrist and hand/finger collision geoms. It is driven by a
   rail/joint force profile, not mocap or teleportation.
6. A separate finite-mass capsule push rod on its own slide rail. The person
   and rod are driven by `qfrc_applied`; their schedule is hidden from the actor.

There is no weld, magnetic grasp, mocap teleport, disabled collision, or visual
marker used to create the contacts. Physics records retain raw MJCF/MJB,
substep metrics, requested action, filtered action and generalized actuator
torque.

## Fair comparison rules

- Use the same command-line fixture, seed, `dt`, duration and assets when
  comparing a baseline.
- Use `dt=5e-5` for formal numbers. `dt=1e-4` is diagnostic only.
- Keep the original `0.2 mm` external penetration gate and task/strict gates.
- Keep canonical48D/action7 dimensions and the shared WBC/action filter.
- Preserve failed episodes; do not tune gates or select away failures.
- Do not feed contact truth, event schedule, ball state, stage, vision or
  distance into a baseline if claiming a proprioceptive comparison.
- The `--relaxed-pillar` option is a separately registered development
  diagnostic. It moves the pillar to `[.620,-.100]`, radius `10 mm`,
  half-height `15 mm`, and softens its material. It is **not** the formal
  combined benchmark and must not be included in the main comparison table.
